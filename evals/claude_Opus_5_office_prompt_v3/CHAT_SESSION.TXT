## Tuning changes I made after observing runs

Four things looked wrong in the first passes, so I changed them:

1. Doors slid so far they hung outside the shaft into the lobby — reduced the slide to 1.05 (still a 1.9-unit clear opening, wider than the four boarding lanes).
2. The car at ~3.9 m/s was so fast no queue ever formed (max 2 riders all day) — set to 0.6 floors/s (~2 m/s), which is realistic and lets groups build.
3. The building filled with 25 visitors by 7:33 AM — added `occupancyDayFactor()` so visitor traffic ramps with the working day and tails off by 19:00, with a small early-bird floor so the scene isn't dead on load.
4. 20 agents spawning in one frame overlapped on the sidewalk — widened the spawn spread to ±4.5 × −1.1/+1.6.

One judgment call worth flagging: I shortened visitor meeting stays to 14–30 min. The prompt's 23% meeting weight is deliberately generous, but combined with long stays it saturated all 20 conference seats and left only 3 workers at desks. Shortening the stay keeps the conference rooms visibly busy (8–16 occupied) while preserving the weight you specified.
