### Key Features & Verification Results

1. **Behavioral & Seating Fidelity**:
   - Seated agents have their hips lowered by `0.35` units to align with chairs/couches and their legs face towards the desk/table (and away from backrests).
   - Seats record explicit facing directions (`sitTargets`) to prevent backwards or inverted sitting positions.

2. **Crowd Navigation & Anti-Bunching**:
   - Arriving agents walk through the physical 3-unit front door threshold on Floor 0 (`outside -> front_door_threshold -> entrance -> lobby_center`).
   - Boarders and threshold traversers are granted temporary crowd repulsion exemptions and stall-recovery fallbacks to eliminate crowd jamming.
