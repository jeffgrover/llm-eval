**What it does**
- A 5-floor cutaway 3D building rendered with a small hand-rolled vanilla-JS perspective renderer on a `<canvas>` (painter's-algorithm box faces, warm windows, slow oscillating camera) — no libraries, no build, works from `file://` with classic scripts only.
- Starts with 4 passengers, one on each of floors 1–4 (F1–F4), floor 5 (F5) empty, cabin parked on the empty floor.
- Repeats the loop: picks a random passenger → cabin eases to their floor → doors slide open → passenger enters (visible inside the glass cabin) → cabin eases to the empty floor → doors open, passenger exits → their old floor becomes the new empty floor → doors close → wait → repeat.
- HUD shows the empty floor, cabin floor, passenger count and live phase; a pulsing green "EMPTY FLOOR" glow marks the empty floor; pause/reset buttons included.
